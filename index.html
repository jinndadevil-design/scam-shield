Why we don't say "LIE": decades of research (24,000+ tested judges, every major method) prove no signal in the face, voice, or body reliably reveals lying — and tools that pretend otherwise mostly accuse honest people. What does work is analyzing the story itself: checkable details, pressure tactics, and evasion. TruthLens shows you what to verify — the truth comes out when you check.
